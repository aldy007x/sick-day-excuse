One line under 200 characters that gets you out of work and gets a laugh — a make.fun bounty entry.

`main.py` writes the excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and self-checks the gate (UTF-8, 1–200 chars, not whitespace-only, single line).

## The entry

> Sorry, can't come in — my bed has filed for custody of me and the judge ruled in its favour.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
